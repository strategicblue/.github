# Strategic Blue

**Forecast, Optimise & Justify your cloud spend**

Strategic Blue is a UK-headquartered cloud FinOps company specialising in rate optimisation and cloud cost forecasting. Since 2010, we have helped organisations unlock significant savings on their cloud infrastructure through intelligent commitment management and predictive analytics.

---

## What We Do

We combine **commodity trading expertise** with **deep cloud forecasting knowledge** to deliver optimal rate optimisation for AWS and Google Cloud customers. Our approach treats cloud commitments as financial instruments, applying sophisticated hedging strategies that maximise savings while preserving flexibility.

### Our Services

- **Rate Optimisation** — Cloud commitment management delivering savings beyond what customers can achieve alone
- **Cloud Forecasting** — AI-assisted cost and usage forecasting to justify commitments and detect anomalies
- **FinOps Advisory** — Expert guidance on cloud financial operations and cost governance
